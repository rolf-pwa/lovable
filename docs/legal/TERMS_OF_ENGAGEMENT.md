# Sovereignty Survey Terms of Engagement

## 1. Scope of Services: The Personal Sovereignty Survey

PWS agrees to provide strategic financial governance and consulting services. The primary objective is the execution of a 90-minute live working session and the creation of a **Personal Sovereignty Survey & Balance Sheet Stabilization Map**.

Services include:

- **Administrative & Emotional Stabilization:** Immediate nervous-system grounding, emergency cash flow checks, and establishing a 30-to-90-day Capital Expenditure Moratorium (pause on major non-essential liquidity decisions).
- **Personal Balance Sheet & Risk Discovery:** Forensic discovery of personal asset alignment, liabilities, spousal priority alignment, and social/family pressure points.
- **Sovereignty Governance Preparation:** Identifying the client's internal "Anchor Net Worth," mapping Storehouse vs. Vineyard capital requirements, and establishing parameters for The Sovereignty Charter.
